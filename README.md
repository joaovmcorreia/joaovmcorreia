# Hi, I'm João 👋

I'm an AI researcher at [Recogna Lab](https://github.com/recogna-nlp) (UNESP, Brazil), with a Master's in Computer Science. These days I work mostly on **interpretability**: I want models to be less opaque, especially in **medicine**, where *why* a model answered matters as much as *what* it answered. I like to think that by looking inside these machines, we also get a glimpse into our own minds.

Robotics was where I started, and I still build robots as a hobby 🤖

## 🤖 On the robotics side

During my undergrad I played IEEE Very Small Size robot soccer with **Carrossel Caipira**, UNESP Bauru's team. I also rebuilt the embedded hardware and software of the Aedromo educational robots. My bachelor's thesis applied assisted deep reinforcement learning (DQN and DDPG) to control differential-drive robots. I keep tinkering with robots in my spare time.
